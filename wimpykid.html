About

This page is dedicated to an essay I wrote in November of 2016, titled "Greg Heffley: A Psychological Analysis." This essay takes a closer examination at the protagonist of the "Diary of a Wimpy Kid" book series by award-winning author Jeff Kinney. It explores the unseen implications of his actions, his role as an unreliable narrator, and the possibility that Greg Heffley may be a sociopath.

This essay was adapted into a video version by YouTuber "Mumkey Jones" in September of 2017, before the video was taken down for unspecified reasons. Before it was removed, it accumulated over four-million views, exposing the disgusting nature of Greg's actions to a massive audience. I believe it may have been taken down for that reason; but regardless, the video version, as well as the original document, are available to be viewed on this website for free.

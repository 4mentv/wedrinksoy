The Lads

  • by Zeepsterd, 2 days, 2 hours ago
  • Journals / Personal
It's been a while since I've been on here. Hell, my last entry was near the end of my Sophomore year of high school. Things have changed since that Tumblr post, so if you really want to read a long journal entry about how life online and in real life has changed, then here it is. The summer of 2015 was really eventful for me online. As I met a few online pals via online. I guess I'll start from the beginning. It was June and I went on a stream of this one guy who was on a podcast a couple times about cartoons. In the chat was this person by the username of Treasurecat. Over time many joined to come and watch that stream and by many I mean like 20 people on a weekly basis.
JULY
It was around July when I met Sketchy and Bernie and things started to heat up for me on Tumblr. Thesketchyscotsman is well... from Scotland and we became pals because one day, Treasurecat, Sketchy, and I stayed after a stream for a long while afterwords and just talked. This is the first time we actually talked. It was like 1 am and we were just talking about stupid things... Sad I know, but it really helped develop The Lads. what is The Lads you may ask? I'll explain that later. Enter Bedhead Bernie. It was the middle of July and this unknown face named "Bedhead Bernie" started watching the streams. I think we really first became friends when he first added me on Skype. How did he find my Skype you may ask? It's not that hard to really find it. So on that night in July, Bernie needed help on a list he was making. That list would evolve more in October, but I'm getting sidetracked. Over time I started to get followers from the stream people on Tumblr. If I remember, it was like late July when i got my 25th follower on Tumblr. Soon i later added Sketchy on Skype and we talked. I then added Treasurecat and things were cool. I was drifting away from a group of friends a bit. I guess I wasn't really invited by a certain group of mine to do things while another group of friends were inviting me to hangout with them all the time. At the end of the month Sketchy and I decided to make a group chat with Bernie and Treasurecat. Sketchy being Scottish and stuff named it "The Lads" on the spot. It was July 31st, 2015 and I had no idea what was going to come from this "Lads" thing, but I was in for a ride.
AUGUST
Summer was shorter this year. Things were going quick and The Lads had just started. A few days after The Lads started, Bernie uploaded lost Shrek Video footage ( )and got 4 million views for it. This was a real accomplishment for Bernie. We also added one lad to the group chat which was Dots. He's the only Lad we added because he was a good friend of ours from the stream chats and was forgotten to be added when Sketchy made the chat. Now let me talk about Bedhead Bernie here for a second... We literally know nothing about him. Bernie is the mystery of the group. Hell we don't even know his real first name or even what he sounds like! Bernie gets people to narrate his videos for him. After that shrek video hit, he got more people to notice him and it's no surprise he's the most popular of The Lads.
Important notes of this month are as followed
* Bernie uploaded his fist episode of his series "The Cutting Room Floor"

*The Lads started to get followed by people and I made it to around 30ish followers in August
*I turned 16 and got some pretty nice fanart from friends. OF course there are people outside of The Lads we would have loved to join, but five is already too much as it is. Such examples are pals Acapellaman (who makes great art!), Harley, Sun, and more!
*I started school at the end of the month and this is when things get different in real life...
SEPTEMBER
This is when things get personal, so Bernie if you're reading this hush up! So school started and I became a Junior. I don't want to get too deep in here, but during September and October I kind of drifted away from a group for certain reasons that aren't that personal, but whatever. Sketchy got more noticed by "famous" people noticing him and so did Tcat. I guess Dots and I are just the ones who sit around and support our friends over here.
Important notes for this month
*Sketchy, bernie, tcat get shit done and Dots and I are there
*real life stuff
*stuff and geometry is fucking hard
* I reach 40ish followers
OCTOBER
This month was really about change. Now change can be good or bad, but what are you going to do. Change comes to all of us in some way or another. I started hanging out with another set of friends of school and I guess that's okay. Other reasons why I moved groups arose, but who really cares. I started appearing on streams with pals and we got into some pretty interesting conversations I probably shouldn't talk about on here. Just saying now, Sketchy is pretty Scottish!
THINGS TO NOTW ABOUT THIS MONTH
*I started working
*I hit 50ish followers this month and now I currently have 58, but that's really small compared to Bernie's high numbers. I also made new online friends too!
* I rendered two videos for Bernie. One is already out and the other one will be released in the next few weeks or something.

*also tons of personal stuff and a goodbye of sorts for a long time for a certain group of friends.
NOVEMBER
"I have no idea what The Lads will be like a year from now, but I think it'll be something we couldn't even imagine" - me a few days after The Lads started. it's only been 3 months and here we are. What the hell will the next few months bring? We've reached the present and now I've decided to do something with the Lads that'll come out by the end of this year. it'll come out on my YouTube channel and hopefully you'll like it whoever is reading this. This school year certainly has been different. it's been a time of change and other things. So I leave you with this drawing Bernie made of The Lads about an inside joke.

That's Bernie's doing. I'll make another entry again. I'm not sure when, but it'll be quite some time from now. (Sketchy on the man, Tcat touching his leg, Dots staring at it, Bernie raising his hands up, and me looking at it and smiling.)
Change is good. Change comes to all of us.
No Comments
